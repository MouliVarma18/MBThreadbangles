Our Creative Process

Crafted With Care

01

Thread Selection & Wrapping

We select premium high-luster silk threads, wrapping them carefully around the bangle base to create a seamless, soft finish.

02

Embellishment Layout

Each design is charted by hand. Kundan gemstones, mirrors, or pearls are symmetrically positioned to create unique patterns.

03

Precision Handwork & Curing

Stones are set using specialized adhesive, followed by natural curing to ensure durability and wear resistance for special occasions.

Simple Step-by-Step Customization

How to Order

01

Share Outfit Photo

Send a photo of your saree, lehenga, or dress in natural light. Highlight the border and primary colors you want to coordinate.

02

Customize Details

Confirm your bangle sizing (e.g., 2.4, 2.6) and discuss custom elements like name-studding, stones, pearls, or matching hair accessories.

03

Artisan Handcrafting

We wrap each thread base and set Kundan stones, mirrors, or pearls symmetrically. The design is cured naturally to ensure high durability.

04

Secure Delivery

Your matching accessories are securely packaged and delivered to your doorstep, ready to complete your festive or bridal look.
